# Company Research

## Steps
1. From official and reputable public sources: what they do, products, size, location, recent news, projects relevant to space/embedded/AI, culture signals, and any reputation or scam warnings.
2. Output one page: summary, why it fits, likely hiring teams, 3-5 talking points, risks or doubts, and sources with dates.
3. Do not state anything uncertain as fact; mark [VERIFY].
