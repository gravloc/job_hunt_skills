# Recruiter Outreach

## Steps
1. Identify 1-2 relevant people from public sources: the recruiter named on the posting, the team lead, or the HR contact on the company site or LinkedIn. Public business information only; no private data.
2. Draft a LinkedIn note (under 300 characters for connection requests) and a longer message or email (under 120 words): who he is, the specific role, one concrete reason for fit, a polite ask. Attach nothing unrequested.
3. No flattery, no pressure, no 'I am the perfect candidate'.
4. Provide two tone options. Save drafts as Gmail drafts or in applications/<company>_<role>/outreach.md.
5. He approves and sends (or approves sending). Log date and person in tracker.csv. One follow-up after 7 days; then stop.
6. Respect no-reply or 'not interested' and add them to do-not-contact.
