# Application Form Fill

## Steps
1. Open the application in the agent's browser (he must already be logged in; ask him to log in if not). Never enter passwords or 2FA.
2. Fill fields from facts.md and answers.md only. For any question not covered, especially visa, salary, demographics or legal declarations, stop and ask him.
3. Upload the tailored CV and letter from this application's folder.
4. Take screenshots of the filled form and review page; save to applications/<company>_<role>/.
5. STOP before the final Submit. Show him a summary of what will be sent.
6. After he approves (or if standing permission covers it), submit once, save the confirmation, update tracker.csv to 'applied'.
7. If the site shows CAPTCHA, odd verification, payment requests or asks to install software, stop and tell him.
