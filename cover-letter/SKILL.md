# Cover Letter

## Steps
1. Read the posting, company page and facts.md. Find 2-3 specific reasons this role and company fit, and 2-3 real achievements that prove fit.
2. Structure (max one page, 250-350 words): why this role/company, what I bring with evidence, what I'd like to contribute, polite close.
3. Honest tone, no cliches, no exaggeration, no copying from the posting. Mention his student status and availability only as stated in answers.md.
4. German companies: formal, concise; English unless the posting asks for German. Never write German text he cannot stand behind; flag it.
5. Save to cover_letters/<company>_<role>.docx and .pdf. Show him for approval.
