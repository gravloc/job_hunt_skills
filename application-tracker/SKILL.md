# Application Tracker

## Steps
1. Keep tracker.csv complete and correct after every action.
2. Summary: counts per status, replies, interviews, applications waiting more than 14 days, follow-ups due.
3. Suggest next actions: who to follow up with, what to prepare, what to stop pursuing.
4. Weekly review: response rate, which roles and letters work best, recommended changes to search or CV.
