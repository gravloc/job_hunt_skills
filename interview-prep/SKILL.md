# Interview Prep

## Steps
1. Read the posting, his tailored CV and letter, and the company research.
2. Prepare: likely technical questions (embedded, sensors, ML/CV, space systems as relevant), behavioural questions with STAR answers drawn from his real experience, 5 questions to ask them, and logistics (time zone, format, tools).
3. Flag topics where his experience is thin and how to answer honestly.
4. Add calendar event drafts. Never accept or reschedule for him without approval.
5. After the interview: notes, follow-up thank-you draft, update tracker.
