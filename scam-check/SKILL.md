# Scam Check

## Steps
1. Verify the company exists and the posting appears on its official site; check the sender's email domain matches.
2. Red flags: fees or equipment purchases, requests for ID/bank details early, only WhatsApp/Telegram contact, vague duties with huge pay, urgency pressure, job offer without interview.
3. Verdict: Safe / Uncertain / Likely scam, with reasons. Add a scam flag in tracker.csv and never engage with likely scams.
